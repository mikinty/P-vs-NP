This page is a continuation of the
**P-versus-NP page** created and maintained for over two decades by
[Dr. Gerhard J. Woeginger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerhard_J._Woeginger),
professor of computer science and combinatorial optimization, who passed away in 2022.

Gerhard catalogued more than one hundred claimed attempts at settling P vs NP —
with patience, dry wit, and meticulous scholarship — and his page became the
standard reference for the strange and wonderful history of this problem.
We are deeply grateful for his work, and this site exists to keep it alive.

His original page now directs to a
[memorial page](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10951-022-00748-4)
published in his honor.

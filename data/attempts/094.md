In September 2012, Sergey V. Yakhontov proved that P=NP.
The proof is constructive, and explicitly gives a polynomial time deterministic algorithm that determines whether
there exists a polynomial-length accepting computational path for a given non-deterministic single-tape Turing machine.
The paper is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1208.0954](http://arxiv.org/abs/1208.0954).

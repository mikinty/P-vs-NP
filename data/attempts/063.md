In June 2010, Han Xiao Wen proved that P=NP.
His paper *"Mirrored Language Structure and Innate Logic of the Human Brain as
a Computable Model of the Oracle Turing Machine"*
suggests an algorithm of relation learning and recognition (RLR) that enables the
deterministic computers to simulate the mechanism of the Oracle Turing machine,
or P=NP in a mathematical term.
This paper is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1006.2495](http://arxiv.org/abs/1006.2495).

In September 2010, Han Xiao Wen provided another proof that P=NP in the
paper *"Knowledge Recognition Algorithm enables P = NP"*.
The paper is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1009.0884](http://arxiv.org/abs/1009.0884).

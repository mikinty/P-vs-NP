In July 2006, Craig Alan Feinstein provided yet another version of his
proof that P is not equal to NP.
The paper is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/cs.CC/0607093](http://arxiv.org/abs/cs.CC/0607093).
The title of the paper is
*"A New and Elegant Argument that P is not NP"*

A response to this proof appeared in June 2007 in the article
[*"Critique of Feinstein's Proof that P is not Equal to NP"*](http://arxiv.org/abs/0706.2035)
by Kyle Sabo, Ryan Schmitt, and Michael Silverman.

The final version of this paper appeared under the titel
[*"An Elegant Argument that P≠NP"*](http://www.ptep-online.com/index_files/2011/PP-25-07.PDF)
in Progress in Physics, 2011, Volume 2, on pages 30-31.

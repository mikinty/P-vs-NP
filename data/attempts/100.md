In October 2013, Frederic Gillet showed that P=NP.
He shows how the implementation of conservative logic gates on flow networks allows us to solve 3SAT
and 3-dimensional matching problems in polynomial time by using standard minimum-cost flow methods.
The paper *"Solving 3-SAT and 3-dimensional matching in polynomial time"*
is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1310.1971](http://arxiv.org/abs/1310.1971).

Addendum. In November 2013, the author added the following comment to the arxive page:
The proposed method does not work. Updated the article with an analysis of why the general method
suggested cannot work. Will withdraw the paper once this updated version is loaded.

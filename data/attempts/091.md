In summer 2012, Junichiro Fukuyama from the Toyota InfoTechnology Center proved that P is not equal to NP.
The proof explores topological properties of a Hamming space, generalizes the sunflower lemma, and builds on circuit theory.
The paper *"Computing Cliques is Intractable"* is available at
[https://junfukuyama.wordpress.com/](https://junfukuyama.wordpress.com/).

In May 2013, the author made his proof also available on the ArXive, at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1305.3218](http://arxiv.org/abs/1305.3218).

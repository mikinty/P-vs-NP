In December 2006, Howard Kleiman proved that P is equal to NP:
[http://arxiv.org/abs/math.CO/0612114](http://arxiv.org/abs/math.CO/0612114).
The title of the paper is
*"The Asymmetric Traveling Salesman Problem"*.
Kleiman uses a modification of the Floyd-Warshall algorithm to solve
the Asymmetric Traveling Salesman Problem in polynomial time.

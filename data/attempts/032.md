In August 2006, Mohamed Mimouni proved P=NP by constructing a polynomial
time algorithm for the clique problem.
His paper (in French) is available at
[http://www.wbabin.net/science/mimouni.pdf](http://www.wbabin.net/science/mimouni.pdf).

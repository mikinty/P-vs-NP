In October 2025, Ben Goertzel — known as an AI researcher and founder of
SingularityNET, an unusually high-profile author in this genre — claimed that
P is not equal to NP. The paper constructs an efficiently samplable family of
SAT instances for which P = NP would force constant conditional
description-length information in witnesses, contradicting an asserted
"evidence-budget" lower bound of Ω(t).
It is available at [arXiv:2510.08814](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08814).

The claim has not been accepted by the research community.

In 2006, Hemant Pandey submitted a manuscript titled
*"P Vs NP... Are they same?"* to Applied Mathematics and Computation.
The manuscript was subsequently withdrawn. An archived copy is preserved
on Zenodo: [doi:10.5281/zenodo.21770323](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21770323).

This entry is kept as a historical attribution and archival record;
it does not claim that the P vs NP problem has been solved.

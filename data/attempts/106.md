In September 2014, Anatoly Panyukov proved P=NP by constructing a polynomial time algorithm
for the Hamiltonian circuit problem.
The paper *"Polynomial solvability of NP-complete problems"* is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1409.0375](http://arxiv.org/abs/1409.0375).

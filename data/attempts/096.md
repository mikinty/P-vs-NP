In December 2012, Louis Coder proved that P=NP.
The proof designs an algorithm that determines the solvability state (solvable
or unsatisfiable) of any exact-3-SAT formula in polynomial time.
The paper *Polynomial Exact-3-SAT Solving Algorithm* is available as pdf on
[http://vixra.org/abs/1212.0109](http://vixra.org/abs/1212.0109).
In December 2013, Louis Coder designed a new version of his algorithm.
The paper *"Polynomial SAT-Solver - Algorithm Explanation"* is available
as pdf on vixra.org as well.

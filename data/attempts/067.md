In November 2010, Vladimir Romanov proved P=NP.
His paper *"Non-Orthodox Combinatorial Models Based on Discordant Structures"*
introduces a novel method for compact representation of sets of n-dimensional
binary sequences in a form of compact triplets structures (CTS), supposing
both logic and arithmetic interpretations of data.
It is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1011.3944](http://arxiv.org/abs/1011.3944).

Additional information can be found at
[http://romvf.wordpress.com/2011/01/19/open-letter/](http://romvf.wordpress.com/2011/01/19/open-letter/).

In April 2015, Alejandro Sanchez Guinea established P=NP by designing
an algorithm which takes a logical formula in conjunctive normal form, terminates
successfully iff the formula is satisfiable, and runs in polynomial time.
This implies P=NP.
The paper *"Understanding SAT is in P"*
is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1504.00337](http://arxiv.org/abs/1504.00337).

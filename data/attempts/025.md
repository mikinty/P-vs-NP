In October 2005, Lokman Kolukisa designed a polynomial time algorithm for
recognizing tautologies.
This implies P=co-NP, and hence also P=NP.
His paper is called *"Two Dimensional Formulas and Tautology Checking"*
and it is available
[here](http://geocities.com/lkoluk2003/).

In January 2011, Ruijia Liao established that P is not equal to NP, by
using algorithms and pseudo-algorithms.
His paper *"The Complexity of 3SAT_N and the P versus NP Problem"* is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1101.2018](http://arxiv.org/abs/1101.2018).

In June 2026, T. S. Arthanari claimed a machine-checked proof that P = NP:
membership in the pedigree polytope (the convex hull of Hamiltonian-cycle
encodings arising from his multistage-insertion formulation of the TSP) is
shown solvable in strongly polynomial time à la Tardos (1986), which — since
symmetric TSP reduces to it — would yield P = NP.
The paper is available at [arXiv:2606.03194](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03194),
with the claimed zero-`sorry` Lean 4 development linked from its abstract.

A formally verified proof of P = NP would be extraordinary, and scrutiny so
far has focused on whether the formulation genuinely encodes TSP as claimed;
independent validation is still pending.

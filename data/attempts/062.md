In June 2010, Carlos Barron-Romero established P not equal to NP.
His paper *"The Complexity Of The NP-Class"* presents a novel and straight formulation,
and gives a complete insight towards the understanding of the complexity of the problems of
the so called NP-Class.
The paper is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1006.2218](http://arxiv.org/abs/1006.2218).

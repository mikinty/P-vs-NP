In October 2011, Jose Ignacio Alvarez-Hamelin proved P equals NP.
He presents an efficient algorithm for computing a maximum clique in general graphs.
His paper *"Is it possible to find the maximum clique in general graphs?"* is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1110.5355](http://arxiv.org/abs/1110.5355).

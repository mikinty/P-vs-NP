Since June 2024 (and still under active revision), Arne Hole has argued that
the P versus NP problem may resolve negatively *under a constructive reading*:
he constructs a family of decision problems for which no "constructible and
sound" theory can verify solution algorithms, and discusses how classical
proofs of NP ⊆ EXPTIME rely on an assumption that may fail constructively.
The paper is available at [arXiv:2406.16843](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16843).

This is an unprovability-flavored argument rather than a direct proof of
either outcome, and it has not been accepted by the research community.

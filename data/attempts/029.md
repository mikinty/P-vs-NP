In 2005, Dr. Joachim Mertz proved P=NP.
His main contribution is a linear programming formulation of the
TSP with O(n^5) variables and O(n^4) constraints.
More information about this approach can be found at
[http://www.merlins-world.de/](http://www.merlins-world.de/).

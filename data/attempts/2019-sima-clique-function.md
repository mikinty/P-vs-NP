In November 2019, Boyu Sima claimed that P is not equal to NP by arguing
that replacing NOT gates with constant 1 in circuits for the clique function
preserves super-polynomial lower bounds — the same strategy (extending
monotone circuit lower bounds to general circuits) that fails for the
[Tardos function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tardos_function), and that
sank Blum's 2017 attempt.
The paper is available at [arXiv:1911.00722](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.00722).

The claim has not been accepted by the research community.

In September 2008, Jorma Jormakka proved that P is not equal to NP by showing that
the subset sum problem cannot be solved in polynomial time.
His paper *"On the existence of polynomial-time algorithms to the subset sum problem"*
is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/0809.4935](http://arxiv.org/abs/0809.4935).

In May 2012, Michel Feldmann established P=NP.
His paper *"From classical versus quantum algorithms to P versus NP"*
reduces 3-SAT to a linear programming problem, while introducing Bayesian probability theory in classical computation.
According to algorithmic complexity theory this proves that P=NP.
The paper is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1205.6658](http://arxiv.org/abs/1205.6658).

In July 2012, Wen-Qi Duan proved that P=NP.
His paper *"A Constructive Algorithm to Prove P=NP"*
first reduces the undirected Hamiltonian cycle problem into the TSP problem with cost 0 or 1, and then
develops an effective algorithm to compute the optimal tour of the transformed TSP.
This yields a polynomial time algorithm for the undirected Hamiltonian cycle problem, and established P=NP.
The paper is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1208.0542](http://arxiv.org/abs/1208.0542).

In November 2005, Francesco Capasso constructed a polynomial-time algorithm
for Circuit-SAT.
This implies P=NP.
The paper is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/cs.CC/0511071](http://arxiv.org/abs/cs.CC/0511071).

Versions 1, 2, and 3 of this paper (that have been uploaded to
the archive, respectively, on Nov 18, 22, and 23) were called
*"A polynomial-time algorithm for Circuit-SAT"*.
From Version 4 (uploaded Nov 28) onwards, the paper is called
*"A polynomial-time heuristic for Circuit-SAT"*.

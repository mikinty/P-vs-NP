In 2006, Khadija Riaz and Malik Sikander Hayat Khiyal proved that P is equal to NP:
[http://www.scialert.net/pdfs/itj/2006/851-859.pdf](http://www.scialert.net/pdfs/itj/2006/851-859.pdf).
The title of their paper is
*"Finding Hamiltonian cycle in polynomial time"*.
The result has been published in the Information Technology Journal 5 (2006), pages 851-859.

In December 2025, Darren J. Edwards posted a 200-page claimed separation of
P from NP, compiling Turing-machine computation into sum-of-squares
polynomials with bounded "entanglement width" and proving rank lower bounds
via Ramanujan–Tseitin expanders — while asserting immunity to the
relativization, natural proofs, and algebrization barriers, and deferring
community verification and machine-checked formalization to future work.
The paper is available at [arXiv:2512.11820](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.11820).

The claim has not been accepted by the research community.

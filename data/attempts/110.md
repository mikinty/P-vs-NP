In October 2015, Yubin Huang proved the following:
For any language L accepted by a non-deterministic Turing machine in polynomial time, there exists a
deterministic Turing machine that accepts L in polynomial time.
A restricted Turing machine, whose maximum number of nondeterministic moves in the accept computation
tree of inputs is limited, is constructed and studied.
This limitation establishes a hierarchy of classes associated with natural numbers between P and NP.
Any language in NP is also accepted by machines in each of these classes, and P forms the lowest hierarchy level.
The paper *"Testing a new idea to solve the P = NP problem with mathematical induction"*
is available at
[https://peerj.com/preprints/1455/](https://peerj.com/preprints/1455/).

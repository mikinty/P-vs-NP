In March 2011, Algirdas Antano Maknickas proved that P=NP.
His paper *"How to solve kSAT in polynomial time"*
uses multi-nary logic analytic formulas in modulo form to derive a polynomial time problem
for the NP-hard k-Satisfiability problem.
The conclusions of the paper are that every mathematical problem is solvable in polynomial time if
exist full, appropriate and correct knowledge basis for it and the time to get each item of knowledge
basis is match less than calculation time on this items.
The paper is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1203.6020](http://arxiv.org/abs/1203.6020).

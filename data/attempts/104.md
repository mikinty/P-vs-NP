In February 2014, Daegene Song established that P is not equal to NP.
The main idea is to consider P (respectively NP) as a class of deterministic (respectively nondeterministic)
polynomial-time physical processes, and to exhibit a physical process in quantum theory which belongs to NP
but cannot be contained in P.
The paper *"The P versus NP Problem in Quantum Physics"* is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1402.6970](http://arxiv.org/abs/1402.6970).

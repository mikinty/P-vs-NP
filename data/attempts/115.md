In summer 2016, Eli Halylaurin showed P=NP.
The main result is that PSPACE is included in P. Because it is already known that P
is included in NP and NP is included in PSPACE, this implies the desired P=NP.
The paper *"An Attempt to Demonstrate P=NP"* is available at
[http://vixra.org/abs/1605.0278](http://vixra.org/abs/1605.0278).

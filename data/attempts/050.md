In December 2008, Javaid Aslam proved that P is equal to NP.
His paper *"The Collapse of the Polynomial Hierarchy: NP=P"*
is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/0812.1385](http://arxiv.org/abs/0812.1385).
Aslam proves that counting of Hamiltonian Circuits in a given graph is in NC,
and that the Polynomial Hierarchy collapses.

Follow-ups:
The paper
["Refutation of Aslam's Proof that NP = P"](http://arxiv.org/abs/0904.3912)
by Frank Ferraro, Garrett Hall, Andrew Wood points out a flaw in the above paper.
Javaid Aslam's reaction to this refutation is available as
["Response to Refutation of Aslam's Proof that NP = P"](http://arxiv.org/abs/0906.5112).

Here is a link to a
[paper](http://arxiv.org/abs/cs.CC/0303008)
by Givi Bolotashvili that proves P=NP.
The title of the paper is
*"Solution of the Linear Ordering Problem (NP=P)"*.
The paper has been archived in March 2003 at the
"arXiv.org e-Print archive".

An earlier version of this paper appeared in 1990 in the
journal "Soobshcheniya Akademii Nauk Gruzinskoi SSR" under the title
*"A polynomial algorithm for a problem of linear orders"*.
This version was written in Russian with an English and a Georgian
summary.

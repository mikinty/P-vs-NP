In March 2013, the International Conference on Advances in Engineering Technologies and Physical Science
was held in Hong Kong.
The proceedings of this conference have been published by the respected publishing house *Springer Science*,
and contain the paper *Linear Programming Formulation of Boolean Satisfiability Problem* by Algirdas Antano Maknickas.
Maknickas translates the (NP-hard) satisfiability problem into a (polynomially solvable) linear programming
problem.
This of course implies P=NP.
The paper is available at
[http://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-94-007-7684-5_22/](http://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-94-007-7684-5_22).

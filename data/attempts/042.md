In March 2008, Cynthia Ann Harlan Krieger applied for a patent for a polynomial time solution for
the (NP-complete) Hamiltonian circuit problem:
[http://www.freepatentsonline.com/y2008/0071849.html](http://www.freepatentsonline.com/y2008/0071849.html).

The full proofs of the underlying theorems are available in the paper
["The Proof of P=NP"](http://teaching.cs.uml.edu/~heines/techrpts/Papers/HC-final.pdf)
by Cynthia Ann Harlan Krieger and Lee K. Jones.

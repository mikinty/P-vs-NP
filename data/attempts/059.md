In 2009, Narendra S. Chaudhari designed a constructive procedure for solving the 3-SAT
problem in polynomial time O(n^13).
The main trick lies in the representation that allows Chaudhari to cast the 3-SAT problem in
terms of the CLAUSES and not the literals.
The complexity is fundamentally different because the mapping from literals to clauses is
exponential while from clauses to 3-SAT is linear.
The paper *"Computationally Difficult Problems: Some Investigations"*
has appeared in the Journal of the Indian Academy of Mathematics Vol 31, 2009, 407-444.
A copy is available
[here](http://dcis.uohyd.ernet.in/~wankarcs/index_files/pdf/Vol-31-No-2-2009-pp407-444-scanned-copy.pdf).

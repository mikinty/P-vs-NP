In November 2006, Raju Renjit proved that co-NP is equal to NP:
[http://arxiv.org/abs/cs.CC/0611147](http://arxiv.org/abs/cs.CC/0611147).
The title of the paper is
*"co-NP Is Equal To NP"*.
By investigating the clique problem, the author recognizes that there exists a
case where the time complexity of NP and co-NP are the same in the worst case.
This result nicely complements an earlier paper by Renjit (from February 2005),
where he proves that P is not equal to NP.

In November 2011, Craig Alan Feinstein proved that P is not equal to NP.
The argument is surprisingly short.
It essentially shows that the Traveling Salesman Problem cannot be solved in polynomial-time on a classical computer.

The paper *"The Computational Complexity of the Traveling Salesman Problem"* is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0611082](http://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0611082).

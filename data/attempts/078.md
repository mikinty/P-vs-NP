In October 2011, Koji Kobayashi showed that P is not equal to NP, by building the language CHAOS in NP\AL.
The paper *"NP is not AL and P is not NC is not NL is not L"* is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1110.0200](http://arxiv.org/abs/1110.0200).

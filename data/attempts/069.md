In December 2010, Carlos Barron-Romero established that P is not equal to NP.
His paper *"The Complexity of Euclidian 2 Dimension Travelling Salesman Problem
versus General Assign Problem, NP is not P"* focuses on the Euclidian 2-dimensional
Travelling Salesman Problem and the General Assignment Problem, and presents the
differences between these two NP problems.
The paper is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1101.0160](http://arxiv.org/abs/1101.0160).

In July 2011, Sergey Kardash proved P=NP by designing an O(n^12) time algorithm for 3-SAT.
His paper *"Algorithmic complexity of pair cleaning method for k-satisfiability problem"*
is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1108.0408](http://arxiv.org/abs/1108.0408).

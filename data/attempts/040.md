In summer 2007, Guohun Zhu proved that P is equal to NP:
[http://arxiv.org/abs/0704.0309v3](http://arxiv.org/abs/0704.0309v3).
The title of the paper is
*"The Complexity of HCP in Digraps with Degree Bound Two"*.
Zhu uses techniques from matching theory to design a polynomial time
solution for the NP-hard Hamiltonian Cycle problem in bipartite cubic graphs.
This [page](http://www.glrq.net/bicubic.html)
contains an outline of the argument.

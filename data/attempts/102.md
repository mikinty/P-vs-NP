In February 2014, Pawan Tamta, B.P. Pande and H.S. Dhami proved P=NP by constructing a polynomial
time algorithm for the clique problem.
They show that the Clique Problem has a reduction to the Maximum Flow Network Interdiction Problem,
and they review the reduction to evolve a polynomial time algorithm for the clique problem.
The paper *"A Polynomial Time Solution to the Clique Problem"* is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1403.1178](http://arxiv.org/abs/1403.1178).

In April 2015, Hector A. Cardenas, Chester Holtz, Maria Janczak, Philip Meyers, Nathaniel S. Potrepka
published a refutation of this approach.
Their paper *"A Refutation of the Clique-Based P=NP Proofs of LaPlante and Tamta-Pande-Dhami"*
is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1504.06890](http://arxiv.org/abs/1504.06890).

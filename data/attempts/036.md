In November 2006, Rubens Ramos Viana proved that P is not equal to NP:
[http://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0612001](http://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0612001).
The title of the paper is
*"Using Disentangled States and Algorithmic Information Theory to Solve the P Versus NP Problem"*.
Viana uses (i) the Chaitin number Omega and (ii) the fact that the general
decomposition of an N-way disentangled state is an irreducible sentence
whose number of coefficients grows in a non-polynomial way with N,
to construct an NP problem that can never be solved in P.

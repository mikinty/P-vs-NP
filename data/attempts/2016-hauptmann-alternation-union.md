In February 2016, Mathias Hauptmann argued that P is not equal to NP.
Assuming P = Σ₂^P, he proves a variant of the McCreight–Meyer Union
Theorem for Σ₂^P and derives a contradiction.
The paper *"On Alternation and the Union Theorem"*
is available at [arXiv:1602.04781](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.04781).

The claim has not been accepted by the research community.

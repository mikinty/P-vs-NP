In 2006, Radoslaw Hofman proved that P is not equal to NP (under the
assumption that deterministic Turing machines only use deterministic
calculation models).
His paper *"Complexity Considerations, cSAT Lower Bound"*
is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/0704.0514](http://arxiv.org/abs/0704.0514).

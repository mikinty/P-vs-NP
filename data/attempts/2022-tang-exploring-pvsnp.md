In September 2022, Jian-Gang Tang claimed that P is not equal to NP by
polynomial-time reducing an EXP-complete problem to a problem in NP,
concluding EXP ⊆ NP and deriving a contradiction with the time hierarchy.
The paper *"Exploring P versus NP"* is available at
[arXiv:2209.15410](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.15410).

The claim has not been accepted by the research community; reductions can
only show EXP-complete problems lie outside P, not inside NP.

In July 2010, Mikhail Katkov established P=NP.
His paper *"Polynomial complexity algorithm for Max-Cut problem"* formulates the
NP-hard Max-Cut problem as a semi-definite program.
The paper is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1007.4257](http://arxiv.org/abs/1007.4257v2).

Additional information on this approach can be found at
[http://mkatkov.wordpress.com](http://mkatkov.wordpress.com/).

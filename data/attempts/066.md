In August 2010, Sergey Gubin proved that the ATSP (Asymmetric Travelling Salesman Polytope)
can be expressed by an asymmetric linear program of polynomial size.
This implies P=NP.
His paper *"Complementary to Yannakakis' theorem"* has been published in Volume 74
of *The Journal of Combinatorial Mathematics and Combinatorial Computing* on pages
313-321.

And here is a
[refutation](http://www.win.tue.nl/~gwoegi/P-versus-NP/review-rizzi.pdf)
of Gubin's arguments by Romeo Rizzi from January 2011.

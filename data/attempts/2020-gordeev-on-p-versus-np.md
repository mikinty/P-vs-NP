In April 2020, Lev Gordeev claimed an extension of Razborov's approximation
method to De Morgan-normal (non-monotone) circuits, showing that CLIQUE
requires super-polynomial deterministic time. Later versions claimed to fix
errors using the Isabelle proof assistant.
The paper *"On P Versus NP"* is available at
[arXiv:2005.00809](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.00809).

In 2021, David Narváez and Patrick Phillips published a dedicated refutation,
identifying a crucial mistake in Lemma 12 of the paper (the approximators only
track positive inputs): see their paper
*"On Lev Gordeev's 'On P versus NP'"* at
[arXiv:2104.07166](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.07166).

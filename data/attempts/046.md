In August 2008, Jerrald Meek proved that some NP-complete problems in fact are not
really NP-complete. Of course this implies that P is not equal to NP.
His paper *"Analysis of the postulates produced by Karp's Theorem"*
is the final article in a series of four articles.
It is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/0808.3222](http://arxiv.org/abs/0808.3222).

Here is a link to the
[paper](http://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0310060)
*"Evidence that P is not equal to NP"*
by Craig Alan Feinstein.
This paper does **not** claim to settle the "P versus NP" question,
but provides some evidence for "P-not-equal-to-NP" in a certain restricted
model of computation.
An earlier
[paper](http://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0305035)
*"P is not equal to NP"*
by the same author has been withdrawn, since a counter-example had been found.
Both papers have been archived in 2003/04 at the "arXiv.org e-Print archive".

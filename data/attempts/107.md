In March 2015, Michael LaPlante established P=NP by constructing a polynomial time algorithm
for the maximum clique problem in a graph.
The paper *"A Polynomial Time Algorithm For Solving Clique Problems"* is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1503.04794](http://arxiv.org/abs/1503.04794).

In April 2015, Hector A. Cardenas, Chester Holtz, Maria Janczak, Philip Meyers, Nathaniel S. Potrepka
published a refutation of this approach.
Their paper *"A Refutation of the Clique-Based P=NP Proofs of LaPlante and Tamta-Pande-Dhami"*
is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1504.06890](http://arxiv.org/abs/1504.06890).

In May 2010, Changlin Wan proved that P=NP.
The central idea of the proof is a recursive definition for Turing machine (shortly TM).
The paper is called *"A Proof for P vs. NP Problem"*,
and is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1005.3010](http://arxiv.org/abs/1005.3010).

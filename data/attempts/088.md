In March 2012, Minseong Kim showed that the Zermelo-Fraenkel axioms of set theory together with the axiom of
choice are inconsistent, and then deduced from this that P is not equal to NP.
The paper
*"Inconsistency of the Zermelo-Fraenkel set theory with the axiom of choice and its effects on the computational
complexity"* is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1203.0494](http://arxiv.org/abs/1203.0494).

In June 2011, Matt Groff established P=NP through an O(n^7) time algorithm for the
satisfiability problem.
The paper *"Towards P = NP via k-SAT: A k-SAT Algorithm Using Linear Algebra on Finite Fields"*
is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1106.0683](http://arxiv.org/abs/1106.0683).

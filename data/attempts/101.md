In January 2014, Hanlin Liu established P=NP by constructing a polynomial time algorithm for the
Hamiltonian circuit problem in a graph G=(V,E); the algorithm has a time complexity of O(|V|^9).
The paper *"A Algorithm for the Hamilton Circuit Problem"* is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/1401.6423](http://arxiv.org/abs/1401.6423).

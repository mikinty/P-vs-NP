In November 2008, Zohreh O. Akbari proved that P is equal to NP.
Her paper *"A Deterministic Polynomial-time Algorithm for the Clique Problem
and the Equality of P and NP Complexity Classes"*
appeared in Volume 35 of the *"Proceedings of World Academy of Science,
Engineering and Technology"*,
and is available at
[http://www.waset.org/pwaset/v35/v35-74.pdf](http://www.waset.org/pwaset/v35/v35-74.pdf).
Akbari designs a deterministic polynomial time algorithm for the NP-hard clique problem.

In 2013, an updated version of the paper was presented at the 2013 IEEE/ACIS 12th
International Conference on Computer and Information Science (ICIS), under the title
*"A polynomial-time algorithm for the maximum clique problem"*.
The paper is available at
[IEEExplore](http://ieeexplore.ieee.org/xpl/login.jsp?tp=&arnumber=6607889).

In fall 2011, Jeffrey W. Holcomb established P!=NP.
The website [http://www.holcombtechnologies.com](http://www.holcombtechnologies.com/) contains
a sequence of papers that together establish P!=NP.
The paper
["Just How Random Are Your Answers?"](http://www.holcombtechnologies.com/Papers/Just%20How%20Random%20are%20Your%20Answers.pdf)
seems to be a particularly important one.
The proof relies upon the existence of stochastic answers in the set difference between NP and P.

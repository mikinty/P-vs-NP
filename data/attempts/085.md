In January 2012, Gilberto Rodrigo Diduch proved that P is not equal to NP.
His paper ["P vs NP"](http://paper.ijcsns.org/07_book/201201/20120124.pdf) states that the
answer to the P vs NP question is a categorical NO; these classes are different as their names suggest.

Diduch's paper has been published in the International Journal of Computer Science and Network Security
([IJCSNS](http://ijcsns.org/)), Volume 12, pages 165-167.

In January 2021, Manoj Kumar proposed a characterization of CNF satisfiability
via "fully populated clauses" and "sibling clauses", deriving from it a claimed
polynomial-time algorithm for SAT and hence P = NP.
The paper is available at [arXiv:2101.05597](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.05597).

In December 2021, Michael C. Chavrimootoo and Henry B. Welles III published a
detailed critique, *"A Critique of Kumar's 'Necessary and Sufficient Condition…'"*,
showing that the proposed algorithm takes exponential steps on infinitely many
inputs: [arXiv:2112.06062](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.06062).

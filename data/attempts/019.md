In January 2005, Andrea Bianchini proved that P is equal to NP, by constructing a polynomial time
exact algorithm for the NP-hard SubsetSum problem.
His paper *"A Polynomial-time Exact Algorithm for the Subset Sum Problem"*
is available as [pdf](http://es-andreabianchini.it/ssp_eng.pdf).

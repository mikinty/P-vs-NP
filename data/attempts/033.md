In October 2006, Sergey Gubin proved P=NP by constructing a polynomial
time algorithm for the directed Hamiltonian cycle problem.
His paper is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/cs.DM/0610042](http://arxiv.org/abs/cs.DM/0610042).
The title of the paper is
*"A Polynomial Time Algorithm for The Traveling Salesman Problem"*.

Here are some
[comments](http://arxiv.org/abs/0704.0514)
by Radoslaw Hofman on this proof.

And here is a full
[refutation](http://arxiv.org/abs/0804.2699)
of Gubin's arguments by Ian Christopher, Dennis Huo, Bryan Jacobs from April 2008.

(Thanks to Juergen Ernst for providing the link to Christopher, Huo, and Jacobs.)

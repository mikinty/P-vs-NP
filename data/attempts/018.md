In November 2004, Mircea Alexandru Popescu Moscu introduced an
invariance principle of complexity hierarchies.
His paper is available at
[http://arxiv.org/abs/cs.CC/0411033](http://arxiv.org/abs/cs.CC/0411033)
and seems to prove that P is not equal to NP, if *"you are willing
to believe that the property of a complexity class to be closed
or openend to the nondeterministic extension operator it's an
invariant of complexity theory"*.
